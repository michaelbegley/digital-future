PRIVACY

Your uploads

Videos and pictures you upload are converted on our server and deleted within an hour, along with the results. Nobody looks at them and they are not shared, unless you add one to the Archive.

The Archive

Adding to the Archive is your choice. When you add something, we check a few frames and the first 15 seconds of sound from your original upload for nudity, violence and hateful or abusive speech. Part of that check is done by an automated service run by OpenAI. Then the site owner reviews the result and a few small frames of the original before anything is shown. The check copies are deleted once a decision is made. What we publish is only the converted version, up to 15 seconds.

Results in the Archive stay public until removed. You can ask us to remove yours at any time (Removal). Anything reported is taken down for another look.

Your number (No. 4F2A91C) comes from a random code your browser keeps. Limits on adding, voting and reporting use a scrambled form of your network address. While something you added is waiting for review we also keep the address itself, and delete it when the review is done. If an upload appears to break the law, we keep it and the address and report it to the authorities.
