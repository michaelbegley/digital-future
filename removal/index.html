Copyright

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with: the work you own, a link to the item, your contact details, a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized, a statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or may act for the owner, and your signature.

Repeat infringers are blocked.

Anything illegal

Tap Report and pick "Sexual or illegal". It comes down at once. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
